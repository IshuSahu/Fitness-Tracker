Protein is the whole game, and your budget is the constraint

The plan lands at 150 g against a 155 g target. Two whey scoops a day would make this easy and cost about ₹3,600/month — over your ₹2,500. So the load sits on soya, paneer, dal and curd, and if a meal slips you lose 25–40 g fast. Below 137 g at 85.4 kg in a 600 kcal deficit, you will lose scale weight and some of it will be arm and chest. Keep 50 g dry soya and 100 g paneer in the house as your two emergency levers.

📉
Your deficit is correctly boring

You have come down from 87 to 85.4 kg, so every number here has been rebuilt around the lighter you — 2,200 against an estimated 2,800 maintenance, which is ~0.55 kg per week. Expect to redo this every 4–5 kg: a smaller body burns less, which is why plateaus arrive on a plan that never changed. Slow enough to keep strength climbing, which is the only reliable proof you are keeping muscle. This is also why I didn’t touch your pre-workout bread and peanut butter: cutting 200 kcal from the meal that fuels your only training window is the worst 200 kcal to cut. If the scale hasn’t moved across three weekly averages, drop 150 kcal or add a 20-minute walk — not both.

🌙
Recovery is your bottleneck, not effort

You asked for 6 days and you clearly can train 6 days. But at ~6.6 h average sleep, a 600 kcal deficit and a 10-to-7 desk job, the ceiling on this plan is recovery. That’s why Thursday sits in the middle of the week rather than at the end, and why it now carries a walk, a stretch circuit and shoulder mobility instead of nothing — easy movement clears more soreness than sitting still does. Built-in rule: if you sleep under 6 h for three nights running, turn Sunday’s Legs B into a 30-minute walk. That is the plan working, not you failing. And a 5:00 AM wake-up on a Legs day is a trade — take it knowingly.

🕤
The 9:45 PM problem is solvable, and mostly already solved

Your whey on the way home already covers post-session protein — the “anabolic window” is hours wide, not minutes, so stop worrying about it. The real issue is a 10 PM dinner roughly two hours before bed. Keep dinner protein and vegetable heavy, low in oil (the soya sabji here is ~10 g fat on purpose) so it digests before you lie down. Your one genuinely useful tweak: a 200 ml milk and roasted chana snack near 11:15 PM costs 175 kcal and buys 10 g of slow protein across a 7-hour fast.

📏
The love handles: no exercise reduces them

Side bends, twists and oblique work build the muscle underneath; they do not remove fat from above it. Waist fat goes when total body fat goes, and the flanks are usually one of the last areas to respond — expect visible change around weeks 8–12, not week 3. Stop weighing yourself for proof and measure your waist at the navel weekly instead. Target: under 90 cm (half your height). The 1.6 kg already gone is mostly not from the flanks yet, and that is exactly what the research predicts. That number, your weekly weight average and your top set on incline press are the only three metrics worth tracking this block.
